- 2026-09-04 05:44 — WHY the sheet can diverge from JT at all (owner: 'should be impossible'). The sheet is NOT a copy of JT — ingestion writes the sheet first, push creates the JT bill and stamps JT Cost Item ID, mirror reconciles only afterwards. The mirror has two deliberate refusals: (1) JT_MIRROR_DELETE_UNLINKED_LINES=false, so a surplus sheet line with an EMPTY JT Cost Item ID is kept and noted, never deleted — rationale is it may be not-yet-pushed, effect is a stray row is immortal; (2) when both sides have unequal leftovers the guard sets Structure Mismatch and touches NO lines, and because that is the if-branch it also skips the header Amount mirror in the else. So a diverged bill is frozen and re-flagged hourly forever. For 689659: sheet 4 CSI groups vs JT 3 lines = unequal leftovers, so guard (2) fires every run.
